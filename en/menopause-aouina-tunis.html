Menopause in L’Aouina: preparing for a consultation

Hot flushes, sleep and cycle changes: a visit to discuss your concerns.

30 / 09 / 2026 · Cabinet Dr Hager Gabsi Boujelbane

A transition experienced differently

Perimenopause can involve cycle changes, hot flushes or sleep difficulties. Symptoms and their impact vary. You can discuss them during a gynecology consultation even before your periods have stopped.

What to prepare

Note your recent period dates, symptoms and how they affect daily life. Bring prescriptions and previous results if available. Write down questions about contraception, intimate comfort and care options.

Care based on your circumstances

Options depend on your medical history and preferences. A consultation provides time to discuss suitable care and any relevant examinations. This article does not recommend an individual treatment.
