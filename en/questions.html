Questions and answers

General information to help prepare a consultation. It does not replace personalized medical advice.

When should I see a gynecologist?

A consultation can help at different ages, depending on your questions or symptoms. A first visit can simply be a conversation without a routine examination.

Gynecology consultations →

What if my periods are painful?

Record the frequency, severity and effect on daily life. Discuss them during a consultation to determine appropriate care.

Gynecology consultations →

When should cervical screening begin?

The test and timing depend on age, medical history and applicable guidance. Ask your gynecologist what suits your situation.

Gynecology consultations →

How do I choose contraception?

Options depend on your health, preferences and plans. A consultation helps compare methods and contraindications.

Gynecology consultations →

When should pregnancy follow-up begin?

Contact the practice after confirming pregnancy to plan a first appointment and prepare your questions and previous tests, if any.

Gynecology consultations →

How can I discuss menopause?

If changes are troubling you, record symptoms and questions to discuss suitable options with your doctor.

Gynecology consultations →

What if I have symptoms of an intimate infection?

Avoid self-diagnosis. Describe the symptoms and their progression to a healthcare professional.

Gynecology consultations →

How can I prevent urinary tract infections?

Advice depends on your situation, especially with recurrent episodes. Seek medical advice for symptoms, particularly fever or significant pain.

Gynecology consultations →

May my partner accompany me?

Ask the practice before the appointment. Your comfort and confidentiality come first.
