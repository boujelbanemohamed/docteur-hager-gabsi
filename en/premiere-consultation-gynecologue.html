When should I see a gynecologist?

A consultation can help at different ages, depending on your questions or symptoms. A first visit can simply be a conversation without a routine examination.

A first visit based on your needs

You can book to discuss periods, contraception or discomfort without waiting for a particular age. Tell the practice this is your first visit and write down what you would like to discuss.

What happens during the visit?

The discussion starts with your concerns and medical history. Ask for an explanation of any proposed examination. An intimate examination is not automatic at a first visit; your consent and comfort matter.
