What should I bring to a gynecology consultation?

Write down your questions and, if available, bring previous test results, a list of medications and relevant medical history. The practice can confirm what is needed when you book.

Useful information to gather

Note your questions, recent period dates and symptoms you wish to discuss. Bring available results, medications and known allergies. You do not need new tests unless requested by your healthcare professional.

Explain your needs

Mention whether this is a first visit or follow-up when booking. Ask about any preparation needed for the planned procedure. You can also share concerns or a need for support.
