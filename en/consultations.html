How to prepare for your appointment

Gather the information you already have to help you discuss your situation with the doctor.

  1. 1

    Practical information

    Explain the reason for your appointment and mention any particular needs when calling.

  2. 2

    Available medical records

    Bring relevant previous reports, test results and assessments.

  3. 3

    Cycle and symptom notes

    Note the dates of your last period, your symptoms and the questions you want to ask.

  4. 4

    Treatments and medical history

    Prepare your medication list, known allergies and relevant medical history.
