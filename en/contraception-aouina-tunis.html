Contraception in L’Aouina: choosing with your gynecologist

A confidential consultation to discuss methods and your needs.

30 / 09 / 2026 · Cabinet Dr Hager Gabsi Boujelbane

There is no single choice for everyone

Pills, intrauterine devices, implants and condoms are among the available methods. Choice depends on your needs, preferences and medical circumstances. Confirm availability and whether a method can be provided at the practice during your appointment.

Useful information to share

Bring your medication list, medical history and experiences with previous methods. Mention breastfeeding, a recent birth or plans for pregnancy. Explain your priorities, such as ease of use or preferred duration.

Questions before deciding

Ask about use, possible effects, follow-up and what to do if problems arise. Condoms are the only contraceptive method that also protects against sexually transmitted infections. This article does not change any prescription.
