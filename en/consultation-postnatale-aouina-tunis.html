After childbirth: preparing a postnatal visit in L’Aouina

Time to discuss recovery and your questions after birth.

30 / 09 / 2026 · Cabinet Dr Hager Gabsi Boujelbane

The mother’s health matters too

A postnatal consultation provides time to discuss physical recovery and emotional well-being. Questions may involve healing, pain, sleep or everyday activities. Follow-up timing is agreed with the team caring for you.

Prepare documents and questions

Bring your birth report, prescriptions and available results. Note concerns and questions. Breastfeeding, contraception and support needs can also be discussed.

Time to be heard

Postnatal care should reflect your circumstances and priorities. Do not wait for a scheduled visit to report a health problem or concern. Contact your care team to discuss the appropriate next step.
